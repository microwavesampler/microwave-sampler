Privacy Policy

Microwave Sampler for Android · Effective 22 November 2026

Microwave Sampler lets you record, edit and arrange audio on your Android device. You can use the app without creating an account. The developer does not receive your audio or projects when you sample, play, save a project in the app or separate stems.

Your microphone and audio files

When you use a microphone recording feature, the app requests Android microphone permission and captures audio for your recording. You can turn that permission off at any time in Android Settings under Apps, Microwave Sampler, Permissions.

When you import a file, the app reads the audio you choose so you can edit and play it. Both Fast and Normal stem separation process the audio on your device. When you export audio, you choose where to save it in Android’s file picker. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider receives the file you save there.

Internet connections

When a stem model is needed, the app downloads it from Hugging Face over HTTPS and checks its integrity before use. The download request does not include your samples, recordings or project files.

The interface can request typefaces from Google Fonts. A portable project export can request a visualizer library from Dreamloop. Opening the About page or this policy connects your browser to GitHub Pages. To deliver the requested content, these services and their hosting providers receive your IP address and may receive technical details such as the requested file and browser or app version.

The app does not display advertising or sell personal data.

Storage, backups and deletion

Microwave Sampler stores saved projects, their audio, preferences and downloaded models in the app’s storage on your device. It uses temporary files while separating stems and exporting audio. To remove a saved project, select it in the app’s project list and choose Delete Project.

To remove all local app data, including projects, preferences and downloaded models, clear Microwave Sampler’s storage in Android Settings. Delete exported files separately in the location where you saved them. Depending on your Android settings, your device’s backup or transfer service may copy eligible app data. You can manage those copies through that service.

Privacy questions

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond to your request. Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the inquiry. You can use the same address to ask for it to be deleted.
